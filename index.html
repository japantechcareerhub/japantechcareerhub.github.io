Questions

Do I need to speak Japanese?

No. Many teams in Japan work in English. Your Japanese level just helps partners show you the right roles.

Is a job guaranteed?

No. We introduce your profile to recruitment partners. Whether you get an offer depends on the partner, the company, and the interviews.

Who are the recruitment partners?

Recruitment agencies in Japan that place international engineers. They're separate companies and handle hiring support under their own licenses.

Can I remove my profile?

Yes. Email us any time and we'll delete it and stop sharing it.

Privacy notice

Japan Tech Career Hub ("we") handles the information you send as follows.

What we collect

The details in this form, the date you sent it, and the page that referred you.

Why we collect it

To introduce you to recruitment partners who place engineers at companies in Japan, to contact you about that, and, if you opt in, to send our newsletter.

Who we share it with

Only recruitment partners, and only when you've ticked the sharing consent. We don't sell your data or publish it.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after your last update, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete your data, or withdraw consent, email info.aokuro@gmail.com.
